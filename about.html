About Prenaya 💛

At Prenaya, we believe little hands can create big smiles. We create fun, easy-to-use DIY activity kits that encourage children to explore their creativity, learn through play, and enjoy making something with their own hands.

Our kits are thoughtfully made using eco-friendly casting materials and are designed to turn screen-free time into happy, creative moments. 🌈🎨
